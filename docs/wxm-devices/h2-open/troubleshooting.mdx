## Bluetooth and the web flasher

### The station is not listed when I click Connect

<div className="explanation small-big">
  <img src={useBaseUrl("img/wxm-devices/helium/no-device-found.jpg")} />
  <span>

Make sure the station has started: press the reset button at the bottom of the weather station once. The LED flashes a few times when it restarts. Then try again from closer.

Use **Chrome** or **Edge** on a computer or an Android phone. Safari, Firefox and iPhone/iPad browsers do not support Web Bluetooth.

</span>

<hr/>

### It asks to pair but fails, or it connects and then nothing happens

Pairing is only allowed in the **first 5 minutes after the station starts**. Restart the station (one short press of the reset button) and connect within 5 minutes.

If your computer or phone paired with this station before a [factory reset](#factory-reset), remove the station from its Bluetooth settings ("Forget" / "Remove device") and pair again.

If the flasher stays on **Discovering…**, disconnect, wait a few seconds and connect again. If Windows shows a pairing prompt, accept it.

<hr/>

### I can't find the station during the firmware update

During a firmware update the station restarts into update mode and appears in the Bluetooth list as **DfuWX** plus six characters (for example **DfuWX467063**), or as **DfuTarg**, not as "WeatherXM WS2001 …". Select that name.

The station stays in update mode for about 2 minutes and keeps its current firmware until the new one is installed. If the update is interrupted, click **Try again** in the flasher to resume. If the 2 minutes have passed, the station restarts with its current firmware: connect again and start the update again.

<hr/>

### The update says the station "already runs a newer firmware"

The station refuses older firmware than it has installed. Nothing was changed; you can keep using the station.

## LoRaWAN

### The station does not join my network

While joining, the red LED breathes (fades in and out). If it keeps breathing:

1. **Gateway coverage:** check that your gateway is online and receives the station. On TTN, open the gateway's **Live data**; on ChirpStack, the gateway's **LoRaWAN frames**. If the gateway sees nothing, the station is out of range: move it, the gateway, or both.
1. **Region:** the region set in the flasher must match your gateway and the device's frequency plan on the network server (for example EU868 everywhere, or US915 with Sub-Band 2).
1. **Keys:** the DevEUI, JoinEUI and AppKey must be identical in the flasher and on the network server. A single wrong character in the AppKey makes the network server drop the join request (TTN shows this in the device's live data).
1. **LoRaWAN version:** register the device as **LoRaWAN 1.0.4** (RP002 1.0.3).

Each time you disconnect the flasher the station restarts and joins again.

<hr/>

### Uplinks arrive but are not decoded

- Install the [WeatherXM decoder](/wxm-devices/h2-open/ttn-and-chirpstack#payload-decoder) as the uplink payload formatter (TTN) or codec (ChirpStack).
- WeatherXM frames arrive on **FPort 99**. Decoders made for the SenseCAP S2120 do not understand them.
- Uplinks on **FPort 3** mean the station still uses the legacy format from older tools: connect it with the web flasher and apply your settings again to switch to the WeatherXM frame.

<hr/>

### Uplinks are missing or TTN warns about airtime

On The Things Network each device may use 30 seconds of airtime per day. Use an upload interval of **5 minutes or more**, and 10 minutes or more when the station is far from the gateway. See [TTN fair use](/wxm-devices/h2-open/ttn-and-chirpstack#the-things-network-ttn).

<hr/>

### I want the station back on the WeatherXM network

Connect with the web flasher and click **Switch back to WeatherXM**. See [Switch back](/wxm-devices/h2-open/connect-to-your-network#switch-back-to-the-weatherxm-network).

<hr/>

## Factory reset

<div className="explanation small-big">
  <span>

A factory reset restores the factory settings, **erases all paired (bonded) computers and phones**, resets the rain counter, and returns the station to its **WeatherXM network profile**. Your own network profile stays stored; to use your network again, apply your settings with the [web flasher](/wxm-devices/h2-open/connect-to-your-network#4-enter-your-network-settings).

:::warning
Remove the station from your computer's or phone's Bluetooth settings **right before** the factory reset, so you can pair again afterwards. After the reset the station restarts and accepts new pairings for 5 minutes.
:::

To perform the factory reset, hold down the middle button on the bottom of the weather station, count to ~10 and release. The LED flashes very quickly to confirm the reset, then the station restarts.
