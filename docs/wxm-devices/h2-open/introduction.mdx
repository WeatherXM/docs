## What is the H2 Open Edition?

The H2 Open Edition is the WeatherXM H2 (WS2001) weather station connected to **your own LoRaWAN network** instead of the WeatherXM network.

The hardware is the same as the Seeed Studio SenseCAP S2120 8-in-1 station, but the H2 runs **WeatherXM's own firmware**, which does more:

- **Every 12 seconds** the station samples all sensors and aggregates them into one uplink per interval.
- **Wind gust** (the highest 12-second peak in the interval) is reported next to the average wind speed.
- **Rain total** (a lifetime counter) and the **maximum rain rate** of the interval are both reported.
- Everything fits in **one compact 24-byte frame**. Periodically the frame is **signed** by the station's secure element, so you can prove that the data came from that station.
- **Your keys, your data.** You choose the JoinEUI and AppKey. The WeatherXM keys stay stored on the station, so it can switch back to the WeatherXM network at any time (firmware V4.5 and later).
- **Firmware updates over Bluetooth** from your browser, using [flasher.weatherxm.com](https://flasher.weatherxm.com).

:::info
While the station is on your own network it is **not** part of the WeatherXM network: it does not earn $WXM rewards and its data does not appear in the WeatherXM app. You can switch it back at any time with the [web flasher](/wxm-devices/h2-open/connect-to-your-network#switch-back-to-the-weatherxm-network).
:::

## Next steps

### Check the box contents

Make sure that everything is in order and you're all set to proceed with station assembly.

<Link to="/wxm-devices/h2-open/contents">View Contents</Link>

### Assemble your weather station

Assemble all parts and make sure all sensors work as expected. For this step, **you will need 6 AA batteries that are not included in the box.**

<Link to="/wxm-devices/h2-open/assemble">View Station Assembly Instructions</Link>

### Connect the station to your network

Use the web flasher at [flasher.weatherxm.com](https://flasher.weatherxm.com) to bring the station to the latest firmware and point it at your network: choose the region, enter your JoinEUI and AppKey, and set the upload interval. **You will need a computer or Android phone with Chrome or Edge and Bluetooth.**

<Link to="/wxm-devices/h2-open/connect-to-your-network">View Connect Instructions</Link>

### Register the station and decode its data

Add the station to The Things Network or ChirpStack, install the WeatherXM payload decoder, and watch the first measurements arrive.

<Link to="/wxm-devices/h2-open/ttn-and-chirpstack">View TTN and ChirpStack Instructions</Link>

### Install your weather station

This is one of the most important steps, as proper installation is key in order to collect valid weather data. For this step, **you will need a pole that is at least 2m/6.5ft and a way to secure it in place (both not included in the box).** Here you will also see the best practices on how to properly install the station according to WMO.

<Link to="/wxm-devices/h2-open/install-weather-station">View Station Installation Instructions</Link>

Powered via a small solar panel.
The maximum communication distance is 2 to 10km depending on the environment and the position of your gateway.

    - Range: -40.0 - 80.0 °C

- #### Air Humidity:

    - Range: 1 - 99% RH

    - Resolution: 1% RH

- #### Rain Hourly Rate:

    - Range: 0 - 450 mm/h
    - Accuracy: ±7%
    - Resolution: 0.254 mm/h

- #### Barometric Pressure:

    - Range: 540 - 1100 hPa
    - Accuracy: ±5 hPa(700 - 1100 hPa) ±8hPa (540 - 699 hPa)
    - Resolution: 1 hPa

- #### Wind Speed:

    - Range: 0 - 50.0 m/s
    - Accuracy: ±0.5 m/s ({'<'}5 m/s) ±10% ({'>'}5 m/s)
    - Resolution: 0.1 m/s

- #### Wind Direction:

    - Range: 0 - 360°
    - Accuracy: ± 8°
    - Resolution: 1°

- #### Light Intensity:

    - Range: 0 - 200000 lux
    - Accuracy: ± 5%
    - Resolution: 1 lux

- #### UV Index:

    - Range: 0 - 16.0
    - Accuracy: ±10%
    - Resolution: 0.1

<h3>LoRaWAN and Data</h3>

- #### LoRaWAN:

    - LoRaWAN 1.0.4, Class A, OTAA (JoinEUI and AppKey chosen by you)
    - Regions: EU868, US915, AU915, AS923, IN865, KR920, RU864

- #### Uplink:

    - One 24-byte frame on FPort 99 per interval (temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed, wind direction, wind gust, rain rate, rain total, light, UV, battery status)
    - Default interval on your network: 5 minutes (within The Things Network fair-use limit), adjustable from 1 to 1440 minutes
    - Periodically signed (ECDSA P-256) by the station's secure element

<h3>General Parameters</h3>

- #### Operating Temperature:
    - -20 to +60 °C (Use of alkaline batteries suggested)
    - -40 to +60 °C (Lithium battery)

- #### Operating Humidity:

    - 0 - 99% RH

- #### Device Weight:

    - 1.5 kg

- #### Certification:

    - CE / FCC / RoHS

- #### Solar Panel:

    -  0.5W (When there is sunlight, solar panels will be used first, otherwise batteries will be used)

- #### Battery Type:

    - AA size, 1.5 V batteries (alkaline or non-rechargeable Lithium battery)

- #### Number of batteries:

    - External battery box: x6 AA batteries

- #### Battery Life:

    - 1 year (solar panel with external battery box, 20 uplinks / hr)

- #### External Battery Box:

    - Support x6 AA 1.5V batteries
    - Cable length: 2 m
    - IP Rating: IPX6

##### * The battery life varies with the upload interval and the distance to the gateway.
