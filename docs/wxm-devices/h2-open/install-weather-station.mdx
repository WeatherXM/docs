## Prerequisites for proper installation
1. Mounting pole of at least 2m (6.56ft) height (not included)
2. Sturdy mounting base or U-bolts to secure in place (included / hardware accessories)
3. Compass or phone GPS for True North direction alignment

:::info
Proper mechanical installation and siting are essential to collect high-quality, representative weather observations. Obstacles, heat plumes, or mechanical vibration can distort temperature, wind, and precipitation readings.
:::

## Best Practices for Station Siting

The values of meteorological parameters measured by a weather station can significantly differ depending on surrounding obstacles and ground surfaces.

### 1. Mounting Pole Height
Mount the station on a pole at least 2m (6.56ft) above the ground or roof level. The maximum recommended pole height is 5m (16.5ft).

### 2. Secure and Tighten the Pole
The pole should be firmly anchored to prevent oscillation in strong winds. Any pole swaying can cause the rain gauge tipping mechanism to trigger false precipitation counts.

    <img src={useBaseUrl("img/wxm-devices/helium/tighten-pole.svg")} alt="Tighten pole diagram" />
</div>

<hr/>

### 3. Installation Location Guidelines

<h4>Away from Heat Sources</h4>
Ensure the sensor array is at least 5m (16.4ft) away from artificial heat sources, chimneys, exhausts, air conditioning condensers, or ventilation ducts that could falsely elevate temperature measurements.

<section class="control">
  <img src={useBaseUrl("img/wxm-devices/helium/away-from-heat-sources.svg")} alt="Away from heat sources" />
</section>

<h4>Clear of Shadows</h4>
Ensure the station is not shaded by nearby buildings or trees during any part of the day. Solar radiation and UV measurements require direct, unshaded sunlight.

<section class="explanation horizontal">
  <div class="explanation">
    <img src={useBaseUrl("img/wxm-devices/ws1000/building-with-pool-in-roof.svg")} alt="Building siting" />
    <img src={useBaseUrl("img/wxm-devices/ws1000/shadow-soft.svg")} alt="Shadow avoidance" />
  </div>
</section>

<h4>Distance from Obstacles</h4>
Position the pole at a distance from surrounding obstacles (trees, walls, roof ridges) at least 2–4 times the height difference between the obstacle and the sensor. For large obstacles (>8m), maintain a distance of 10–15 times the obstacle height when possible.

<hr/>

### 4. Direction Alignment & Leveling

<div class="explanation">
    <img src={useBaseUrl("/img/wxm-devices/helium/direction-alignment.svg")} alt="Direction alignment" />
    <p>
    Install the weather station in an open location with no obstructions above and around the sensor.
    Locate the <strong>North (N)</strong> marker embossed on the top casing of the weather station. Using a compass or phone GPS, rotate the entire assembly so the marker points precisely towards True North.
    <br/><br/>
    Check the integrated <strong>spirit bubble level</strong> on top of the sensor body. Ensure the bubble is centered before tightening mounting pole clamps to prevent rain gauge measurement tilt error and guarantee accurate wind direction tracking.
    </p>
</div>
