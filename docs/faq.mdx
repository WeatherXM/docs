## <Highlight color="#68a4f9">Data</Highlight>

### Is there a chance for my host/friend to be able to have access to my station's data?

Yes! You can share your station's live data by sharing its page from the [WeatherXM Explorer](https://explorer.weatherxm.com/). Anyone can also follow or favorite public stations in the **[WeatherXM Web App](https://app.weatherxm.com)** or Mobile App so they appear in their station list.

<hr />

### How do you know people are not faking the location of their station on the map?

In addition to cryptographic proofs from all station data sent to the WeatherXM Network, we run a dedicated Proof of Location (PoL) algorithm to verify station location accuracy and consistency. Read more in the [PoL documentation](/rewards/proof-of-location).

## <Highlight color="#68a4f9">Hardware</Highlight>

### Can I connect a 3rd party weather station to WeatherXM?

WeatherXM stations use custom firmware and hardware security components designed to sign and transmit verified observations to the WeatherXM Network, so standard 3rd-party consumer weather stations are not directly supported. For open-network LoRaWAN integrations, check out our [H2 Open documentation](/wxm-devices/h2-open/introduction).

<hr />

### What are the differences between WeatherXM station models?

WeatherXM offers weather stations across three connectivity families depending on where you deploy:

- **Wi-Fi (M5 / D1):** An outdoor sensor array paired with an indoor Wi-Fi gateway and display — ideal for homes, offices, and rooftops with Wi-Fi and power.
- **Cellular 4G (Pulse):** Built-in 4G LTE connectivity with solar power for remote or off-grid sites without Wi-Fi.
- **LoRaWAN / Helium (H1 / H2 / H2 Open):** Standalone solar-powered outdoor stations using LoRaWAN connectivity — great for areas with Helium or LoRaWAN network coverage.

For a full side-by-side comparison and current availability, visit **[weatherxm.com/stations](https://weatherxm.com/stations)**.

<hr />

### Can I connect my WeatherXM Helium station to any Helium gateway around, or should I request access?

You can deploy your WeatherXM Helium station anywhere with Helium network coverage. Packets are automatically routed through whichever nearby Helium gateway receives them best — you do not need to request permission or manually pair with a specific gateway.

<hr />

### Will it make a big difference if I place my station on the balcony instead of the roof?

Yes. Placing your station on an unobstructed rooftop (or open mast) rather than a balcony is critical because nearby walls, overhangs, and heat-reflecting surfaces degrade temperature, wind, solar, and rain accuracy, which directly reduces your [Data Quality (QoD v3)](/rewards/data-quality) score and daily rewards.
Check our [Deployment Examples](/wxm-devices/deployment-examples) for best practices.

<hr />

### Which is the recommended frequency for my country?

You can check the regional frequency mapping table on the [Frequency Plans](/wxm-devices/frequency-plans) page.

## <Highlight color="#68a4f9">Rewards</Highlight>

### How can I start receiving rewards?

Once you have installed and claimed your station, connect an Arbitrum One-compatible EVM wallet (`0x…`) via the **[WeatherXM Web App](https://app.weatherxm.com/profile)** or Mobile App ([wallet setup guide](/mobile-app/wallet/add-edit-wallet-address)), and then follow our [How to Claim Rewards](/rewards/rewards-claiming) guide.

<hr />

### How can I check my rewards?

You can inspect daily station rewards, streaks, and QoD/PoL/SPV breakdowns in the **[WeatherXM Web App](https://app.weatherxm.com)** (**Rewards** tab) or the Mobile App, and claim accumulated `$WXM` tokens at [claim.weatherxm.com](https://claim.weatherxm.com).

<hr />

### Do I have to pay for the HNT/DC fees?

No — WeatherXM covers the Helium Data Credit (DC) fees.

<hr />

### Can I change my wallet address in the future?

Yes. Daily rewards are allocated to whichever wallet address is connected at the time of distribution. If you switch wallets, any previously allocated unclaimed rewards remain claimable by your previous wallet and cannot be transferred to the new wallet.

<hr />

### How many stations are allowed in a Hex?

Each H3 cell has a predefined **[Cell Capacity (CC)](/rewards/cell-capacity)** based on its geospatial characteristics. You can check any cell's capacity and current active stations on the [WeatherXM Explorer](https://explorer.weatherxm.com/). If more eligible stations are active than the cell's capacity, the highest-scoring stations (and earliest claimed in case of a tie) receive rewards.
