### Installing on Solana Mobile (Seeker)

WeatherXM is natively listed on the **Solana dApp Store**:

1. Open the **Solana dApp Store** app on your **Solana Seeker** device.
2. Search for **WeatherXM** (or browse the **DePIN** category).
3. Tap **Install** and sign in with your WeatherXM account — all station management, Bluetooth onboarding, SPV photo uploads, and rewards features work identically to the Google Play build.

## What You Can Do in the Mobile Apps

- **Station Claiming & Onboarding:** Pair and claim **D1**, **Pulse**, **H1/H2**, and **M5** stations via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi provisioning.
- **Station Photo Verification (SPV):** Capture and upload the four directional installation photos directly from your phone camera during setup.
- **Helium / LoRaWAN Firmware Updates:** Perform over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates over Bluetooth for Helium stations.
- **Live Conditions & Hyperlocal Forecasts:** View real-time temperature, humidity, wind speed/gusts, pressure, precipitation, solar radiation, UV index, and hourly/daily forecasts for your stations or any public station worldwide.
- **Daily Rewards & Claiming:** Inspect daily QoD v3 scores, reward streaks, and claim accumulated `$WXM` tokens directly to your Arbitrum One wallet.

:::tip Prefer a Desktop Cockpit?
For multi-station fleet management, deep historical charts, and the **Health** offline debugger on desktop or browser, use the **[WeatherXM Web App (app.weatherxm.com)](/mobile-app/web-app)** with the same account.
:::
