The **[WeatherXM Web App](https://app.weatherxm.com)** is the browser-based **Station Cockpit** for monitoring your weather stations, inspecting historical data and forecasts, tracking daily WXM rewards and Data Quality (QoD), and diagnosing offline or underperforming hardware — from any desktop or mobile browser.

Sign in at **[app.weatherxm.com](https://app.weatherxm.com)** using the same account email and password you use in the WeatherXM iOS or Android app.

## Fleet Rail & Station Cockpit

On desktop, the Web App uses a split-pane layout:

- **Fleet Rail (left):** Shows all your owned and followed stations grouped by status (**Needs attention** at the top, followed by **Healthy · Owned** and **Following**), with live temperatures, offline duration, latest daily rewards, and instant search by station name or serial.
- **Station Cockpit (right):** Displays the selected station's live status, firmware version, H3 cell rank, and 7 focused tabs.

## Station Tabs

| Tab | Available To | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Owned & Followed | Live weather conditions, 24-hour temperature trend chart, 5-day thermal range forecast, hardware & signal summary, and a 30-day rewards bar strip. |
| **Forecast** | Owned & Followed | Hyperlocal hourly and 5-day forecast with multi-element switcher (temperature, wind, precipitation, pressure, solar/UV) and 3-hourly breakdown. |
| **History** | Owned & Followed | Day-by-day historical charts for temperature (with feels-like & dew point), humidity, wind speed & gust (plus wind direction), pressure, precipitation, and solar/UV. |
| **Rewards** | Owned only | Latest day, 30-day, and lifetime WXM totals, interactive 30-day bar chart, and per-day breakdown of **Base reward**, **Data Quality (QoD)**, **Boosts**, and **Cell position**. |
| **Health** | Owned only | Full **Offline Station Debugger** separating outdoor station health from gateway health, live signal chain, battery, firmware status, GPS lock, and one-click support diagnostics. |
| **Photos** | Owned only | View your uploaded **Station Photo Verification (SPV)** photos alongside deployment guidelines. |
| **Settings** | Owned & Followed | Set a personal friendly name for the station, view hardware metadata, or unfollow a followed station. |

### Diagnosing Issues with the Health Tab

The **Health** tab visualizes the exact path your data travels — **Outdoor Station → Gateway → WeatherXM Cloud** — so you can immediately tell whether an outage is on the outdoor sensor unit (e.g., low battery or RF link) or on the indoor gateway (e.g., Wi-Fi/4G connectivity, firmware, or GPS lock on M5).

If you need help from Support, click **Copy diagnostics** to copy a structured hardware and telemetry summary to your clipboard.

### Tracking Rewards & Data Quality

The **Rewards** tab shows your daily WXM earnings across the last 30 days. Clicking any bar opens that day's breakdown — including your QoD score, active boosts, cell rank, and any reward-reducing or advisory quality annotations.

<img src={useBaseUrl("/img/web-app/station-rewards.png")} alt="WeatherXM Web App Rewards Breakdown" />

### Exploring Historical Weather Data

The **History** tab lets you step through any past date in the station's local timezone and inspect multi-parameter charts for temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, precipitation, and solar irradiance.

## Account, Wallet & Preferences

From the bottom of the Fleet Rail or your profile avatar, you can also manage your account directly in the browser:

- **Profile (`/profile`):** View your account details, add or update your EVM rewards wallet address, or initiate account deletion.
- **Settings (`/settings`):** Choose your preferred measurement units (temperature, wind speed, pressure, precipitation) and light/dark/system appearance.
- **Password Reset (the **Forgot Password** flow):** Request a password reset email when logged out.

## Web App vs Mobile App

Most day-to-day monitoring, diagnostics, and account management tasks work seamlessly in **both** the Web App and the Mobile App. A few hardware-proximity actions remain exclusive to the iOS/Android Mobile App:

| Action | Web App (`app.weatherxm.com`) | Mobile App (iOS / Android) |
|---|---|---|
| Monitor live weather, 5-day forecast & history | Yes | Yes |
| Fleet management & multi-station search | Yes (Split-Pane Fleet Rail) | Yes |
| Hardware Health & Offline Station Debugger | Yes (with Copy Diagnostics) | Basic status |
| View daily rewards, QoD score & annotations | Yes | Yes |
| Add / edit EVM wallet address | Yes (`/profile`) | Yes |
| Change password / delete account | Yes | Yes |
| Configure units & dark/light theme | Yes (`/settings`) | Yes |
| **Claim & onboard a new station (Bluetooth / QR)** | Redirects to Mobile App QR (`/claim`) | **Yes** |
| **Relocate an existing station** | No | **Yes** |
| **Upload new SPV installation photos** | View gallery & guide | **Yes** |
