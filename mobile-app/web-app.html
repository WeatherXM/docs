WeatherXM Web App (app.weatherxm.com)

The WeatherXM Web App is the browser-based Station Cockpit for monitoring your weather stations, inspecting historical data and forecasts, tracking daily WXM rewards and Data Quality (QoD), and diagnosing offline or underperforming hardware — from any desktop or mobile browser.

Sign in at app.weatherxm.com using the same account email and password you use in the WeatherXM iOS or Android app.

Fleet Rail & Station Cockpit​

On desktop, the Web App uses a split-pane layout:

  • Fleet Rail (left): Shows all your owned and followed stations grouped by status (Needs attention at the top, followed by Healthy · Owned and Following), with live temperatures, offline duration, latest daily rewards, and instant search by station name or serial.
  • Station Cockpit (right): Displays the selected station's live status, firmware version, H3 cell rank, and 7 focused tabs.

Diagnosing Issues with the Health Tab​

The Health tab visualizes the exact path your data travels — Outdoor Station → Gateway → WeatherXM Cloud — so you can immediately tell whether an outage is on the outdoor sensor unit (e.g., low battery or RF link) or on the indoor gateway (e.g., Wi-Fi/4G connectivity, firmware, or GPS lock on M5).

If you need help from Support, click Copy diagnostics to copy a structured hardware and telemetry summary to your clipboard.

Tracking Rewards & Data Quality​

The Rewards tab shows your daily WXM earnings across the last 30 days. Clicking any bar opens that day's breakdown — including your QoD score, active boosts, cell rank, and any reward-reducing or advisory quality annotations.

Exploring Historical Weather Data​

The History tab lets you step through any past date in the station's local timezone and inspect multi-parameter charts for temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, precipitation, and solar irradiance.

Account, Wallet & Preferences​

From the bottom of the Fleet Rail or your profile avatar, you can also manage your account directly in the browser:

  • Profile (/profile): View your account details, add or update your EVM rewards wallet address, or initiate account deletion.
  • Settings (/settings): Choose your preferred measurement units (temperature, wind speed, pressure, precipitation) and light/dark/system appearance.
  • Password Reset (/forgotpassword): Request a password reset email when logged out.
