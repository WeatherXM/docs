Data Quality

info

WeatherXM runs Quality of Data (QoD) v3 (version 3.0.0) — a deterministic, single-day meteorological quality engine with neighbour cross-validation and 2-day confirmation hysteresis.

Data Quality (QoD) is the daily algorithm that evaluates the meteorological reliability of weather data reported by every station in the WeatherXM network. Its daily score (0 to 1, or 0% to 100%) directly measures confidence in your station's readings and serves two purposes:

  1. Reliable Weather Data: Identifies high-confidence station observations for WeatherXM forecasts, analytics, and commercial data products.
  2. Fair Daily Rewards: Encourages proper outdoor installation and sensor maintenance so well-deployed stations consistently earn maximum rewards.

You can inspect your station's daily QoD score and any quality annotations in the WeatherXM Web App (Rewards tab) or the WeatherXM Mobile App.

How QoD v3 Evaluates Your Station​

Every day (for the previous UTC day), QoD v3 evaluates your station across four complementary layers:

1. Self-Sanity Checks (OBC & SQC)​

Checks performed on your station's own telemetry stream for every meteorological sensor (temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed, wind direction, precipitation, and solar irradiance/illuminance):

  • Out-of-Bounds Check (OBC): Flags values outside the sensor's physical or manufacturer specifications.
  • Data Completeness (NO_DATA): Measures how many expected packets arrived based on your station model's transmission cadence.
  • Suspicious Spikes (SPIKE_INST): Flags physically unrealistic jumps between consecutive readings (gap-aware so brief transmission drops do not cause false positives).
  • Flatline / Stagnation (LONG_CONST): Flags sensors stuck at an unchanging value for an unrealistic duration.

2. Spatial Neighbour Cross-Validation (CQC)​

Compares your station against trusted nearby WeatherXM stations using adaptive search radii (25 km → 50 km → 100 km):

  • Temperature & Pressure Drift (TEMP_DRIFT, PRESSURE_DRIFT): Detects persistent multi-degree temperature bias or broken/drifting barometers that simple range checks cannot catch.
  • Elevation-Safe Synoptic Barometer Tracking: Mountain stations or stations with coarse elevation estimates are not unfairly penalized for a steady altitude offset as long as they track hour-by-hour synoptic pressure changes accurately (they receive an informational PRESSURE_OFFSET_ADVISORY instead).
  • Stuck Anemometer & Clogged Rain Gauge Checks: Cross-checks zero wind during regional windy events and zero rain during regional storms against wet/windy neighbours.

3. Deployment Classification & 2-Day Hysteresis​

Evaluates whether a station is deployed outdoors, indoors, or surrounded by heavy obstacles using daylight solar irradiance ratios, wind exposure, and temperature/humidity dynamics:

  • No more 7-day lag: Unlike legacy QoD v1 (which used a trailing 7-day window and kept stations penalized for nearly a week after fixing an issue), QoD v3 runs daily with 2-day confirmation hysteresis.
  • 2 consecutive days to confirm, 2 clean days to clear: A single bad day emits an advisory flag only (zero deployment penalty). Two consecutive days of INDOORS or BAD_DEPLOYMENT confirm the penalty (INDOORS_CONFIRMED sets daily QoD to 0; BAD_DEPLOYMENT_CONFIRMED applies a 0.5× multiplier). Once you fix the station, 2 consecutive clean days clear the penalty.
  • Sub-Zero Winter Protection: When daily average temperature is ≤ 0°C, freezing vetoes prevent false INDOORS or WIND_STUCK penalties caused by frozen anemometers.

4. Separation of QoD and Station Photo Verification (SPV)​

In QoD v3, QoD measures purely meteorological data quality. Station Photo Verification (SPV) is applied separately inside the Reward Mechanism (for example, SPV_INVALID applies a 0.5× reward multiplier in the rewarding pipeline without altering your station's underlying meteorological QoD score).

Score-Reducing vs Advisory Annotations​

Not every annotation reduces your rewards. QoD v3 explicitly separates score-reducing flags from advisory (informational) flags:

CategoryAnnotation CodesImpact on QoD Score
Score-Reducing (Sensor)NO_DATA, OBC, SPIKE_INST, LONG_CONST, TEMP_DRIFT, PRESSURE_DRIFT, PROBLEMATIC_ILL_SENSORReduces the affected sensor's daily score
Score-Reducing (Deployment)INDOORS_CONFIRMED (0×), BAD_DEPLOYMENT_CONFIRMED (0.5×)Reduces overall daily QoD after 2 consecutive confirmed days
Advisory / InformationalT_OVERHEAT, WIND_STUCK, BLOCKED_RAIN, PRESSURE_OFFSET_ADVISORY, PRESSURE_REFERENCE_UNCERTAIN, INDOORS (day 1), BAD_DEPLOYMENT (day 1), SPV_INVALID, SPV_OBSTRUCTION, NO_LOCATION, NO_ELEVATION, LOW_SUN, ISOLATEDNo QoD penalty — early warnings to help you inspect your station before hardware or placement degrades
tip

If you see an annotation on your station, open the Health tab in app.weatherxm.com or check the Rewards Troubleshooting guide for concrete fixes.

Data Quality Threshold​

Each day, a station's Data Quality score is a number in [0, 1]. Your daily reward scales directly with this score (combined with Proof of Location, Cell Capacity, and Station Photo Verification).
