# How to change password to your account

Canonical: https://docs.weatherxm.com/mobile-app/change-password

You can reset your account's password using either the **[WeatherXM Web App](https://app.weatherxm.com/forgotpassword)** or the **WeatherXM Mobile App**.

## Via Web App (`app.weatherxm.com`)

- Go to **[app.weatherxm.com/forgotpassword](https://app.weatherxm.com/forgotpassword)** (or click **Forgot password?** on the login screen).
- Enter your account's email address and submit.
- Open the reset link sent to your email to choose a new password.

## Via Mobile App

There are two ways to reset your password in the mobile app depending on whether you are logged in or out.

:::caution
The confirmation email to change your account password normally arrives within some minutes.

If you haven't received the email, contact our support team at [Support](https://support.weatherxm.com/hc/en-us)

Please, do not forget to  also check your **spam** folder.
:::

### Logged in
- Go to My profile, then choose "Preferences & Settings" and press "Change Password" button, enter your account's email. You will receive an email with a link to change your password.

  

### Logged out

- Open the App and the login page must appear, press "Forgot password" button, enter your account's email. You will receive an email with a link to change your password.

  
  
:::note
- **The instructions above are for both versions of the App (Android and iOS).**
:::
